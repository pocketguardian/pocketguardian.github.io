Key Features

Built for the edge cases
other apps ignore.

Device Administrator access, 24/7 watchdog service, and a complete event log with photos — designed to survive aggressive battery management on Xiaomi, Samsung, and every Android in between.

  • 🛡

    Device Administrator

    Intercepts wrong PINs directly on the Android lock screen via the standard DeviceAdminReceiver API. Works even when fingerprint or Face ID is active.

  • 👁

    Watchdog 24/7

    AlarmManager-based heartbeat checks every 5 minutes. Survives forced kills, Doze mode, battery optimization, and reboots. Tested on Xiaomi HyperOS.

  • 📷

    Choose your camera

    Front camera to capture the intruder's face, or rear camera to document the surrounding environment. Your choice, globally in Settings.

  • 🏷

    Photo overlays

    Burn date/time, device model, and Android ID directly onto each photo at capture time. Useful evidence when reviewing Drive backups.

  • 📊

    Statistics & filters

    Filter the event log by type and date range. Statistics screen shows bar charts by day, hourly activity, and a breakdown by event type.

  • 🔑

    PBKDF2 PIN protection

    Your PIN is never stored in plain text. Military-grade PBKDF2-HMAC key derivation with a random 16-byte salt. Secret recovery question as a fallback.

  • ☁

    Google Drive backup Premium

    Photos and event log synced after every event. Data is safely stored on your own Google Drive — even if the phone is lost, stolen, or factory reset.

  • 🔔

    Alarm mode Premium

    Full-volume ringtone on any event. Stops only when you open the app. An audible deterrent that discourages further access attempts.

Photo Quality

Three modes for every need.

Choose between compact files for fast sync or maximum resolution for clear evidence. Switch at any time in Settings.

Eco

Smallest file size. Ideal for slow or expensive mobile data and limited storage. Syncs to Drive in seconds even on 3G.

High

Maximum resolution and sharpness. Clear evidence when you need to identify a face or share with authorities.

Standard

Balanced quality and file size. Face clearly visible, instant sync. Good all-round choice for daily monitoring.

Privacy First

Your data stays with you.

Zero data sent to our servers. Ever. We don't run a backend. We have no access to your photos, logs, or PIN.

  • Firebase Crashlytics collects only anonymous crash data — no photos, no logs, no PIN
